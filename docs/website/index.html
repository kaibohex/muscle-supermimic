Overview

SuperMimic trains a whole-body tracking policy by backpropagating through rollouts of one learned world model of the body. muscle-supermimic runs it on musculoskeletal humanoids: MS-Human-700 (700 muscles) and MuscleMimic's MyoFullBody (354 muscles), on mjlab and MuJoCo-Warp, with MuscleMimic's tracking task.

Between the policy and the skeleton sit activation dynamics, wrapping tendons and Hill-type force curves. Every observation, reward and termination is one analytic function, evaluated by the simulator and on imagined states alike, with a torch replica of MuJoCo's muscles in it. On MyoFullBody, one run trains in 10.7 hours on one GPU to a normalized return of 0.77 on MuscleMimic's 108 held-out clips, 101 of them tracked to the end. On these bodies the policy gradient through imagination can also explode, and we found where: in a closed loop through the policy's muscle-velocity observation. With a per-trajectory clip of that gradient, MS-Human-700 learns as well: 0.61 on the held-out clips at iteration 3000, in a run still training.

One MDP, simulated and imagined

The simulator collects transitions with the current policy plus exploration noise; a world model learns the body's next state from them. The policy then improves through 32 imagined steps: every step it reads its observation, sets the excitations, the activations follow MuJoCo's own activation dynamics, and the world model moves the body. Rewards, terminations and observations on imagined states come from the same functions that label the simulator's transitions, including muscle length, velocity and force from a torch replica of MuJoCo's spatial tendons (equal to MuJoCo's to 10⁻⁹ / 10⁻⁸ / 10⁻⁶). The gradient of the return, with a critic's value at the last step, flows back through all of it.

The task is MuscleMimic's: 100 Hz control, an observation of the full body state with muscle length, velocity, force, excitation and activation and four foot-touch values, a goal of the reference's next frames, the MimicReward on 17 tracked sites, and terminations when the body strays more than 1 m or 1 rad from the reference. No randomization yet. Details: MDP, training.

Where the policy gradient explodes

The policy's gradient passes through 32 steps of world model and policy. Without a remedy, on MS-Human-700 its norm before clipping reached 10²–10⁵ in most updates (a run that learns sits near 2), and the run never took off. Measured on a checkpoint of that run, nearly all of it comes from 1 % of the imagined trajectories, spread over 208 different clips. Going back through the steps, the gradient on those trajectories' state grows about 1.3× per step; on the other 99 % it barely grows.

Gradient of a trajectory's return with respect to its imagined state
median norm per trajectory, 8 × 4096 imagined trajectories (MS-Human-700, unclipped run, iteration 6000)

The world model alone is stable: cutting the gradient through the policy's whole observation brings the norm down to 1.8. Cutting it through one observation term at a time finds the loop: muscle velocity alone takes it from 49K to 16. Muscle velocity feeds the policy, the policy sets the activations, the world model turns them into joint velocity, which is muscle velocity again; on some trajectories the gain around this loop exceeds 1. MS-Human-700 has twice MyoFullBody's muscles, with smaller velocities (median standard deviation 0.022 against 0.053 m/s), and its policy's total gain on them comes out about 2.8 times MyoFullBody's.

Policy-gradient norm with the gradient through one observation term cut
same checkpoint, median of 3 batches; blue: the muscle-state terms of the loop

MyoFullBody is not immune: the run above collapsed the same way at iteration 12700, when its gradient norm jumped from about 1 to 10³–10⁴ and its episodes fell from 280 to about 110 steps.

The remedy.Clipping, at every imagined step, each trajectory's gradient on the state it carries forward (--agent.state-grad-clip 100) bounds this growth without removing the loop. Only 0.1–0.4 % of trajectory steps get clipped, and the gradient norm stays near 1–2 (at most 5). With it, MS-Human-700 takes off around iteration 2000 in both seeds (above), and a new MyoFullBody run reaches 250-step episodes by iteration 3000, against 104 without.

Get started

git clone --recursive https://github.com/kaibohex/muscle-supermimic.git
cd muscle-supermimic && uv sync

# MuscleMimic's retargeted KIT clips (gated: accept the terms on the dataset page first)
uvx --from huggingface_hub hf download amathislab/musclemimic-retargeted --repo-type dataset \
    --include "MyoFullBody/gmr/KIT/*" --local-dir data/musclemimic

uv run train Mjlab-SuperMimic-Flat-MyoFullBody \
    --env.commands.motion.motion-file data/musclemimic/kit_kinesis_train.txt \
    --env.sim.mujoco.integrator euler --env.sim.mujoco.iterations 100 --env.sim.mujoco.ls-iterations 50

MS-Human-700, evaluation, rendering and memory settings: see the README and docs.
