Overview

SuperMimic trains a whole-body tracking policy by backpropagating through rollouts of one learned world model of the body. muscle-supermimic runs it on musculoskeletal humanoids: MS-Human-700 (700 muscles) and MyoSuite's MyoFullBody (354 muscles), on mjlab and MuJoCo-Warp, with MuscleMimic's tracking task.

Between the policy and the skeleton sit activation dynamics, wrapping tendons and Hill-type force curves. Every observation, reward and termination is one analytic function, evaluated by the simulator and on imagined states alike, with a torch replica of MuJoCo's muscles in it. On MS-Human-700, one run on one GPU reaches a normalized return of 0.83 on MuscleMimic's 108 held-out clips in 4 hours (iteration 6500), 99 of them tracked to the end, and 0.89 after 5.2 hours, 107 tracked; it is still training, and MyoFullBody is training with the same code.

One MDP, simulated and imagined

The simulator collects transitions with the current policy plus exploration noise; a world model learns the body's next state from them. The policy then improves through 32 imagined steps: every step it reads its observation, sets the excitations, the activations follow MuJoCo's own activation dynamics, and the world model moves the body. Rewards, terminations and observations on imagined states come from the same functions that label the simulator's transitions, including muscle length, velocity and force from a torch replica of MuJoCo's spatial tendons (equal to MuJoCo's to 10⁻⁹ / 10⁻⁸ / 10⁻⁶). The gradient of the return, with a critic's value at the last step, flows back through all of it. At every imagined step, each trajectory's gradient on the state it carries forward is clipped to a norm of 100 (--agent.state-grad-clip 100): through the policy's muscle-velocity observation the loop can amplify it step after step, and the clip bounds that growth without cutting the loop.

The task is MuscleMimic's: 100 Hz control, an observation of the full body state with muscle length, velocity, force, excitation and activation and four foot-touch values, a goal of the reference's next frames, the MimicReward on 17 tracked sites, and terminations when the body strays more than 1 m or 1 rad from the reference. No randomization yet. Details: MDP, training.

Get started

git clone --recursive https://github.com/kaibohex/muscle-supermimic.git
cd muscle-supermimic && uv sync

# MuscleMimic's retargeted KIT clips (gated: accept the terms on the dataset page first)
uvx --from huggingface_hub hf download amathislab/musclemimic-retargeted --repo-type dataset \
    --include "MyoFullBody/gmr/KIT/*" --local-dir data/musclemimic

uv run train Mjlab-SuperMimic-Flat-MyoFullBody \
    --env.commands.motion.motion-file data/musclemimic/kit_kinesis_train.txt \
    --env.sim.mujoco.integrator euler --env.sim.mujoco.iterations 100 --env.sim.mujoco.ls-iterations 50 \
    --agent.state-grad-clip 100

MS-Human-700, evaluation, rendering and memory settings: see the README and docs.
